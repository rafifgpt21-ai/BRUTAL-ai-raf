# Glossary

These terms describe the Fieldcraft game in `games/Fieldcraft`, built on BRUTAL.

- **Abyssal Realm**: Fieldcraft's enclosed, procedurally generated underground world. New worlds use generator version 5; historical surface worlds retain their original generator.
- **Glimmerveil Grotto**: upper cavern ecosystem of cyan and violet fungi and luminous moss.
- **Sunken Crystal Chasm**: middle-depth crystal caverns with magenta and gold deposits and underground water.
- **Embercore Foundry**: deep obsidian and magma ecosystem.
- **Runic Catacombs**: deep ruins with emerald rune masonry.
- **World breath**: the repeating dimming and brightening of natural luminous materials, driven by saved world time instead of a sun cycle.
- **Resolve / ketenangan**: temporary exploration safety meter restored by portable or environmental light. Exhaustion returns the player to the base.
- **Chunk**: a streamed 16 x 128 x 16 voxel region, addressed in global X/Z coordinates.
- **Item**: a recognizable 16 x 16 pixel-art silhouette, including the tools shown in the hand by shallow cuboid extrusion.
- **Object**: a model with freeform triangle geometry and original pixel textures, such as a faceted crystal, lantern, decorative cache, or workbench. Its world ownership, editing, selection, and collision still follow voxel cells.
- **Crystal shard**: a pointed, faceted mesh spanning one contiguous vertical run of Amethyst or Sun Quartz. Its height follows the deposit; ceiling-supported shards point downward.
